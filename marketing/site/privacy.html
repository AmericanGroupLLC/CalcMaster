Privacy Policy

Effective date: 2026-01-01 · Last updated: 2026-05-22

CalcMaster ("we", "us", "the app") is published by CalcMaster Inc. This policy explains what data the app collects, how it's used, and your choices. We've designed CalcMaster so that the answer to "what do you do with my data?" is, in nearly every case, "nothing — it never leaves your device".

1. Information we collect

On-device data. Conversions, calculations, saved notes, selected region, and notification preferences are stored locally on your device using the OS's standard secure storage (SharedPreferences on Android, NSUserDefaults on iOS). This data is never transmitted to us.

Network calls. When you open the Currency converter, the app requests current exchange rates from api.frankfurter.app (a free public service operated by the European Central Bank). Your IP address is visible to that service. No other identifying information is sent.

Analytics. When enabled in a release build, we collect anonymous usage events: which tab you opened, which calculator you used, whether a subscription was viewed. These events do not contain personally identifying information.

Subscription receipts. Purchases are processed by Apple App Store or Google Play. We do not see your payment card details. We do see whether a given anonymous device identifier has an active entitlement, via RevenueCat, in order to remove ads.

2. Information we do NOT collect

  • Your name, email, or phone number
  • Your contacts, photos, microphone, or camera
  • Your location, except temporarily when you explicitly tap "Use my location" in the GPS Coordinates tool. The coordinates are never transmitted; they're shown to you and then forgotten.
  • Any data that uniquely identifies you across other apps or services

3. Advertising

If you use the free tier of CalcMaster, you may see ads served by Google AdMob. Ad targeting follows Google's standard policies. To opt out of ad personalization, see your device's privacy settings or upgrade to CalcMaster Pro.

4. Children

CalcMaster does not knowingly collect data from children under 13. The app is suitable for all ages and does not contain user-generated content visible to others.

5. Your rights (GDPR / CCPA)

Because we don't store personally identifiable data on our servers, there is nothing to retrieve, correct, or delete on request — uninstalling the app erases everything. To request more information email support@safecodeg.com.

6. Contact

Questions? Email support@safecodeg.com.
